ABOUT REJIG LABS

We build what we recommend.

Rejig Labs is an AI implementation partner based in Phuket, Thailand. We turn AI plans into systems that run inside real businesses, then hand them to the people who use them.

Why Rejig exists.

Most companies don't lack AI tools. They lack someone who connects those tools to how the business really works.

We kept seeing the same pattern: a strategy deck, a few new subscriptions, and nothing different on Monday morning. Rejig Labs exists to close that gap. Fewer slides. More systems that run.

FOUNDER

Rut Adikari

Founder & CEO · known to clients as Mr. RT

Rut started Rejig Labs to give businesses the part most AI advice leaves out: the build.

Before Rejig, Rut worked in management and marketing with companies in logistics, energy, robotics, food and Web3. That time on the business side of the table is the edge. Rut knows where hours and money leak, and that a tool only matters if people use it every day.

Today Rut leads every Rejig project personally, from the first call to the day your team runs the system on its own. Rut also founded Beem, the AI front desk that grew out of Rejig's client work.

What does Rejig Labs do?

Rejig Labs is an AI implementation partner. It audits how a business runs, builds AI systems into the tools the team already uses (voice agents, chat, CRM automation, content engines, knowledge assistants) and trains the team to run them.

Who founded Rejig Labs?

Rejig Labs was founded by Rut Adikari, known to clients as Mr. RT, who leads every project personally.

Where is Rejig Labs based?

Rejig Labs is based in Phuket and Bangkok, Thailand, and works with clients worldwide in English, Thai and Russian.
