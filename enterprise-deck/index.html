We raise this before you have to

Recordings and transcripts On US infrastructure A storage bucket we or you own, in Singapore
Speech to text Region not pinned Pinned to Azure Southeast Asia, subject to vendor confirmation

Three separate fixes, not one switch, and one of them needs an enterprise contract with the voice vendor. The realistic target is kept in Singapore, not kept in Thailand: no vendor in this stack offers Thailand-resident storage today. Thai PDPA permits cross-border transfer with adequate safeguards, so this is usually a policy conversation rather than a legal one. Residency work is scoped and quoted separately, after a short paid discovery.
