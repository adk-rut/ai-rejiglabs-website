THE COST OF AN UNANSWERED PHONE

Every call you can't pick up books somewhere else.

Your phone rings while your hands are full, while the shop is closed, while the whole team is with customers. The caller doesn't leave a voicemail. They call the next place on Google Maps. Hiring a receptionist for one phone line is expensive; asking staff to juggle calls and customers means both get worse. An AI receptionist answers every call the same way, every time, at any hour.
