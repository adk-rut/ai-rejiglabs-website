THE FRONT DESK CAN'T BE IN TWO PLACES

Someone is always checking in, so the phone keeps ringing.

A clinic's front desk juggles arrivals, payments and the practitioner's next patient, and the phone rings through all of it. A caller who reaches nobody does not try again in an hour: they book the next clinic on Google Maps. After closing, the line is dead, which is exactly when working patients finally have time to call. And every no-show that a reminder would have caught is a slot you paid a practitioner to sit through empty.

BUILT FOR HOW A CLINIC ACTUALLY RUNS

Not a generic bot. A clinic front desk.

Booking a clinic is not booking a table. The slot length depends on the treatment, the practitioner matters, and some calls must reach a human immediately.

01Books the practitioner they asked forPatients name their doctor or dentist. The AI checks that person's own schedule, not the clinic average, so it never promises a slot that practitioner doesn't have.
02Knows a cleaning is not a root canalEvery treatment gets its real duration. The AI books the right amount of time, so the schedule stays honest instead of running 40 minutes late by noon.
03Answers the questions that clog your linePrices, opening hours, directions, parking, and preparation instructions like "no food before the blood test". The routine calls stop reaching your staff at all.
04New patients arrive pre-registeredName, phone number and reason for visit are captured on the call and waiting in the calendar note, so the front desk isn't spelling out a surname at check-in.
05Thai, English and Russian on one numberIt picks the language from the caller's first words. Thai patients, expats and medical tourists all book on the same line, with no language menu.
06Chases the no-shows before they happenConfirmations and reminders go out on LINE and SMS, and rescheduling takes one reply. Patients fix their booking instead of silently skipping it.
07Never plays doctorIt gives no medical advice, ever. When a caller describes symptoms or sounds urgent, it follows your escalation rules: transfer to staff in hours, your emergency instructions after hours.
08Recognises returning patientsA returning patient is recognised by their number on the first ring, greeted by name, and skips straight to the booking.
